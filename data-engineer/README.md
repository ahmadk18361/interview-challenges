# Data Engineer

As a **Data Engineer** at Wilcore, you'll design and build the pipelines, systems, and infrastructure that enable data-driven decision making across federal agencies. You’ll work with large, complex datasets to ensure data is clean, accessible, and usable—helping turn raw information into actionable insight for critical government programs.
